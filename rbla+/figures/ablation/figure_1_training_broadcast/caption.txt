Displayed datasets: CIFAR10 and MNLI. QQP source records are retained but are not plotted.

Training-path and broadcast ablation on (a) CIFAR10 and (b) MNLI. Each legend entry denotes training path / evaluated broadcast. Red and blue identify the RBLA and RBLA+ training paths, respectively; solid and dashed lines identify RBLA prefix and RBLA+ spectral broadcasts. All four curves are shown without smoothing. Each point is the unweighted mean accuracy of ten client models evaluated on the full held-out set after broadcast. Cross-broadcast evaluations use the same current-round pre-spectral aggregate within a training path and do not feed back into that path. RBLA+ includes bidirectional RankScale; it is the identity on the CIFAR10 CNN scaling path. Each trajectory uses seed 42, correct rank assignment, and Dirichlet alpha=0.4. The horizontal axis uses logged rounds 0--100, retaining all 101 evaluations. Logged round 0 is the first post-training evaluation, not an untrained initialization. These are single-seed trajectories, without across-seed uncertainty bands. Held-out evaluation uses the test split for CIFAR10 and the validation split for MNLI. Panel-specific vertical limits are used. Full-width server-model accuracy is not plotted.
