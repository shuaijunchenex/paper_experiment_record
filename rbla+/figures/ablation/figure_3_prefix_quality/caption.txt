Displayed datasets: CIFAR10 and MNLI. QQP source records are retained but are not plotted.

Raw-prefix reconstruction quality along RBLA and RBLA+ training paths on (a) CIFAR10 and (b) MNLI. Markers show checkpoint-wise medians of (||X-X_prefix,q||_F^2 - ||X-X_dense,q||_F^2) / ||X||_F^2, evaluated in FP64. Error bars show the 25th--75th percentiles over all recorded layer/unique-target-width pairs with q<Q: 54 and 68 pairs per checkpoint for CIFAR10 and MNLI, respectively, covering all 7 and 17 LoRA layers. Repeated client requests for the same width within a layer are not duplicated. Full factor width is excluded, while non-full widths with a zero optimal residual are retained. The horizontal axis spans logged rounds 0--100. The measured checkpoints are rounds 9, 49, and 100; no measurement at round 0 is added. Lines connect measured checkpoint medians only. Bars describe variation across layer/width pairs, not confidence intervals or seed variability. The y-axis is symmetric-logarithmic with a linear region below 1e-5, allowing zero-valued quartiles to be shown. Only tolerance-sized negative summary values are displayed at zero; signed raw data and quantiles are retained. Both paths use a single seed (42); RBLA+ includes bidirectional RankScale. Reference matrices differ across training paths, so this is a trajectory comparison rather than a same-matrix intervention. Lower excess indicates that an existing raw prefix is closer to its own dense optimum; it does not imply a monotonic reduction of predictive loss.
