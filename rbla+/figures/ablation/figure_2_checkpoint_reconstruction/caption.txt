Reconstruction of real RBLA aggregates at the final checkpoint, logged round 100 (update 101). Panels show (a) CIFAR10, (b) MNLI, and (c) QQP. For each model the structural middle LoRA layer is selected by sorting the recorded layer_index values and taking zero-based position floor(L/2), independently of reconstruction errors: conv2b for CIFAR10 and model.bert.encoder.layer.0.output.dense for both language tasks. The matricized updates have shapes 192 x 192, 128 x 512, and 128 x 512, with aggregate factor widths Q=192, 8, and 8. Every recorded target width q for the selected layer is shown, including the full width. For the convolutional layer q=3r, where r is its logical LoRA rank. The vertical axis is ||X-X_q||_F / ||X||_F for the identical pre-spectral aggregate X=BA within each panel. Raw factor prefixes, compact QR/core-SVD recompression, and dense SVD truncation are compared in FP64. The dense reference uses economy full SVD followed by truncation. The black dashed dense optimum overlaps the blue compact curve at numerical precision. No additional gauge transformation is applied. SP+ denotes only the spectral recompression operator in this offline diagnostic, not a separate training-method baseline. Data are reused from the completed reconstruction experiment; no new decomposition or training is run for this figure.
