## shared_R32_eta0

Synthetic RBLA aggregation experiment. Shared reference factors plus independent client perturbations (eta=0); reference entries are independent Gaussian variables, and noise standard deviation equals the RMS of the corresponding reference block. N=10, m=n=512, R=32, client ranks=[8, 8, 12, 12, 16, 16, 20, 24, 28, 32], and uniform client weights p_i=1/10. Curves show pointwise medians over 30 independent random seeds; shaded bands show the 25th-75th percentiles (IQR), not confidence intervals. Both redistribution methods are paired on the same single RBLA factor aggregation per seed, with effective LoRA scaling=1 and no additional post-aggregation gauge transformation, rotation, permutation, or reordering. All errors use the unsquared Frobenius norm and the target X=B_global A_global. Panel (a) plots relative reconstruction errors and the optimal rank-r reference; the dashed black reference overlaps the measured blue SP+ curve. Panel (b) plots excess errors above that reference, normalized by ||X||_F. Markers identify ranks actually assigned to clients; the full r=1,...,R scan is diagnostic. Only tolerance-level negative excess errors are displayed as zero; raw values are retained.
